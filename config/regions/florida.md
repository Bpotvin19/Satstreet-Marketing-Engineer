## Florida / U.S. expansion watch items

As of August 2026:

- Florida Office of Financial Regulation regulates money transmitters under Florida's money-services-business framework.
- New Florida stablecoin provisions become effective October 1, 2026 for qualifying state-regulated stablecoin issuers.
- Florida has also established a stablecoin pilot framework for certain government payments.
- U.S. federal and state requirements may overlap.

**These facts are market-intelligence inputs, not a legal conclusion about Satstreet's ability to operate in Florida. Legal/compliance approval is required before commercial activity.**
