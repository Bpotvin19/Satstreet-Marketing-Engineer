## What Satstreet is

An OTC trading desk for Bitcoin and digital assets. Clients buy and sell with
institutional-grade execution, same-day settlement, and a dedicated human
trading desk. Trade sizes run from $50,000 to $100M+.

The core promise is a desk, not an app: "from first call to settled trade in
hours, not days."

## Products and services

- **OTC trade desk** — institutional pricing, $50K to $100M+, best-in-class liquidity
- **Same-day settlement** — wire, EFT, CAD or USD
- **Custody options** — institutional custody, self-custody, or multi-sig, matched to the client
- **Portfolio tracking** — balances, positions, trade history through the web app
- **White-glove service** — a dedicated human trading desk, available around the clock

Minimums: no minimum on a conversation, $50,000 minimum on a trade. Personal and
business accounts; most clients approved within 24 hours.

## Editorial guardrail

Standing rule from the content calendar, and the single most useful line in it:

> Historical anniversaries are ideal for education, but any market value, ETF
> flow, AUM, transaction count, adoption statistic or regulatory statement must
> be refreshed and sourced at the time of publication.

Historical facts are stable and safe to state. Live numbers are neither — they
get refreshed and sourced on the morning of publication, by a human, every time.

## Copy frameworks

Structures, not final copy. Refresh every fact before publishing.

**Historical anniversary** — On [DATE] in [YEAR], [EVENT] happened. At the time,
[EARLY CONTEXT]. Today, [CURRENT CONTEXT]. The bigger lesson is not how much the
asset has appreciated — it is how quickly open monetary networks move from
experimentation to global infrastructure. [SATSTREET TAKEAWAY].

**Data-led institutional post** — [EVENT] is a useful moment to measure how far
digital assets have progressed. Since [MILESTONE], [METRIC 1], [METRIC 2] and
[METRIC 3] have changed materially. The direction is clear: access, liquidity
and infrastructure continue to mature. [CTA OR QUESTION].

**Conference preview** — This week the industry gathers in [LOCATION] for
[EVENT]. Three themes we are watching: 1) [THEME], 2) [THEME], 3) [THEME]. The

**Conference recap** — After [EVENT], one message stood out: [CORE TAKEAWAY].
The most important developments were [1], [2] and [3]. For investors and

**Community engagement** — Crypto history question: [QUESTION]. Vote below, then
share the reason for your answer. We will post the full story and the
institutional takeaway tomorrow.

## Conference content sequence

- Two weeks before: "what we are watching" post with 3–5 themes
- Day before: speaker, agenda or networking preview
- During: concise reaction to material announcements — not every headline
- Within 48 hours after: "five takeaways" carousel or client note
- One week after: connect the strongest theme to Satstreet's value proposition

## Brand voice

The site's register is confident, concrete, and unexcited. Short declarative
sentences. Numbers instead of adjectives. It sounds like a desk, not a startup.

Write like that:

- Lead with the concrete thing. "Same-day settlement, CAD or USD" beats "seamless experience."
- No exclamation marks, no emoji clusters, no crypto slang.
- Never hype price. Satstreet makes money on execution, not on people being excited.
- Explain a mechanism rather than repeating a headline.
- It is fine to be plain. "We move $50K to $100M+ and it settles today" is the whole pitch.

## Things we do not say publicly

Start here; add to it every time something makes you wince.

- Never state or imply a price forecast, target, or expected return.
- Never claim assets are "insured" or "guaranteed" without naming the specific
  policy or provider, and never at all without sign-off.
- Never claim a regulatory status, licence, or registration beyond what is
  already stated on satstreet.com — this includes anything about the US.
- Never give investment advice or address a reader as though we know their
  position, mandate, or tax situation.
- Never name a competitor comparatively.
- Never disclose a client, a trade, a size, or anything that could identify one.
