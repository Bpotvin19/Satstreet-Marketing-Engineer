        Satstreet OS · Internal operating system

        Research once. Use it across the firm.

        Satstreet OS turns scattered market information into a shared daily view, then carries that intelligence into trading conversations, client service, sales outreach and content—without removing human judgment.

        One source of truthNotion
        One daily decision layerMacro Desk

        How information moves through the system

        The dashboard is the decision layer between raw information and the work the team performs.

        01 · CAPTURE

        Monitor the market

        Macro, crypto, regulation, geopolitics, market prices and social signals are scanned through defined research windows.

        02 · STRUCTURE

        Write to Notion

        Daily Intel, client resources and content knowledge are organized in one source of truth rather than scattered across tabs and chats.

        03 · DECIDE

        Prioritize for Satstreet

        The Macro Desk ranks Today's Five, identifies the desk implication and separates confirmed developments from noise.

        04 · ACTIVATE

        Put it to work

        The same approved intelligence supports trading, client conversations, outreach, social content and the Thursday newsletter.

        A normal day

        Operating rhythm
        1. Before 8:30The research process reviews the defined macro and crypto sources and writes the morning Macro Desk into Notion.
        2. MorningThe team scans the reference tape, Today's Five, Satstreet so-what and the seven-day catalyst calendar.
        3. During daySales and trading use the same talking points; material developments can trigger an updated brief.
        4. End of dayClient questions, desk feedback and useful signals return to the knowledge layer for the next cycle.

        A normal week

        Compounding workflow
        1. MON–WEDBuild the market narrative, capture recurring client questions and identify the strongest themes.
        2. THURSDAYTurn the week's approved intelligence into inputs for Mike's newsletter and supporting social content.
        3. FRIDAYReview what generated conversations, refine the system and set the following week's priorities.
        4. ONGOINGExpand the knowledge base while keeping client-facing language and internal intelligence clearly separated.

        This is not another dashboard. It is a shared workflow for turning information into coordinated action.
