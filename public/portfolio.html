How this is estimated. Each receipt from outside your tracked wallets is treated as an acquisition priced at the BTC daily close on the day it confirmed. Transfers between two wallets you track are internal — cost basis moves with the coins and no gain is realized. Outgoing sends to outside addresses are matched to lots first-in-first-out to realize P&L. Realized and unrealized are kept separate. Unconfirmed transactions are excluded until they confirm.

Formula. Unrealized = current balance × current price − remaining cost basis. Realized = Σ(proceeds − FIFO cost) on external sends. Total P&L = realized + unrealized. Return % = total P&L ÷ cost basis.

Why "estimated". Blockchain history cannot tell a purchase from a gift, an internal move or coins from another custodian. Where a receipt predates the available price history its basis is unknown and the figure is marked partial rather than guessed. Use Reconcile on a wallet to correct a classification or acquisition price.

Source. Balances and history: mempool.space. Historical and current BTC price: Coinbase BTC-USD daily close. This is not a Satstreet statement of account and does not reflect holdings held with Satstreet.
