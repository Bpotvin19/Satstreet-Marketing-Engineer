Add a public Bitcoin address for a live estimate, or open a clearly labelled sample to review the P&L experience without sharing an address.

Estimated P&L is not tax reporting or a statement of account. Incoming transfers are treated as acquisitions at a historical daily reference price and outgoing transfers use FIFO. This can differ materially from actual cost basis, especially when Bitcoin moved between wallets that are not all tracked here.

Completeness controls. P&L is withheld when the fetched transaction history is truncated, historical pricing is missing, or reconstructed holdings do not reconcile to the current confirmed balance.

Privacy. Addresses and labels are stored only in local browser storage. Address and transaction requests go directly from this browser to mempool.space; only date ranges, never wallet addresses, are used to request historical Coinbase prices.

Indicative data only. Fiat values use third-party reference data and may be delayed or unavailable. Nothing on this page is investment, accounting or tax advice.
