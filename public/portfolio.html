Confirmed balance is the settled on-chain balance of each address: funds received less funds spent, counting only transactions included in a block.

Unconfirmed activity is shown separately and is never added to the confirmed figure. It reflects transactions currently in the mempool, which have not been mined and may be replaced or never confirm.

Fiat values are indicative. They convert the confirmed balance at a third-party reference rate, are not a quote, not an offer, and not the price at which Satstreet will execute. CAD is derived from the USD reference rate.

Source and storage. Blockchain data is requested directly from mempool.space by this browser. Addresses and labels are stored in this browser's local storage on this device only; they are not transmitted to Satstreet and are not recoverable if the browser's data is cleared.

This is not a statement of account. Figures reflect public addresses you chose to watch and do not represent any holdings, positions or balances held with Satstreet.
