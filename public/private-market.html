Opportunity

Desk-assisted execution. Nothing trades on this platform. If you express matching interest, the Satstreet desk contacts each party separately, confirms eligibility, and — only if both sides agree — arranges pricing, settlement and custody through Satstreet's usual OTC process.

This opportunity is indicative and non-binding, and any transaction remains subject to eligibility, compliance review, available liquidity and agreed execution terms.

This prototype does not contact anyone. In the live service, this would notify your relationship manager and reference this opportunity.
