Opportunity

Desk-assisted execution. Nothing trades on this platform. If you express matching interest, the Satstreet desk contacts each party separately, confirms eligibility, and — only if both sides agree — introduces them and arranges any digital-asset or fiat leg through Satstreet's usual OTC process. Inspection, appraisal, title transfer, insurance and delivery of the asset itself remain with the parties and their own advisers — and for real property, with licensed local representation.

This opportunity is indicative and non-binding, and any transaction remains subject to eligibility, compliance review, independent appraisal and inspection, provenance and title verification, available liquidity and agreed execution terms.

This prototype does not contact anyone. In the live service, this would notify your relationship manager and reference this opportunity.
